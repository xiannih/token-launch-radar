    How the numbers are made

    One formula produces every implied value on this board. The fit is done at run time from the snapshot's own 128 live-token protocols; nothing is hardcoded per name. The full record — formula, priors, calibration, audit and limits — is in METHOD.md, shipped with this dashboard.

    Curator TVL is nested inside the protocols it allocates to, so it is counted twice. Every pre-token row in the Risk Curators and Onchain Capital Allocator categories is halved — a structural rule by category, never by name.

    What this radar cannot do

    • The fit is weak: the median prediction is off by 3.4×, and the 80% range on any row spans ×11.4 either way. Treat every value as an order of magnitude, not a price.
    • Odds are backward-looking peer rates. They say how often protocols of that age have launched, not whether this one will.
    • Gas prices and the farmer's share of a launch are assumptions, stated in METHOD.md — change them and the farm verdicts move.
    • The curator discount is a structural prior, not a fitted number. It is the one judgement call in the model, and it is shown, not hidden.
